The swap picture is a cliff edge, working as designed — for now

Compressed swap sits near-full (11.8 of 12 GB at generation) while the disk swapfile is barely touched. Read correctly, this is the design functioning: the chronic working set exceeds physical RAM, and roughly three gigabytes-per-gigabyte compression is absorbing the excess without touching disk. But the margin is gone — the next sustained pressure episode spills to the NVMe swapfile at raw-disk latency, which is precisely the regime where the stall calendar darkens. The system is stable but has no headroom; the tuning programme's budget work is not optional polish.
