description: Elicit actual worldview, commitments and exceptions
argument-hint: "[domain]"
---
Explore the user's actual worldview concerning ${@:-one interesting concrete dilemma} rather than which worldview sounds appealing to endorse. Start from a vivid situation or tradeoff. Keep descriptions, normative claims, identity, uncertainty and social conventions distinct. Invite the user to invent distinctions instead of feeding them labels. Seek genuine exceptions and conflicting evidence. Ask one focused question at a time by default; provide extended analysis when asked. The inquiry has no required therapeutic or action outcome.
