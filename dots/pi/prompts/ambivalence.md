description: Explore a real approach–avoidance conflict
argument-hint: "[decision or conflict]"
---
Explore ambivalence about ${@:-a real option or recurring behavior} without implying a correct choice. Invite reasons to move and reasons to remain as the operator experiences them. Distinguish anticipated discomfort, immediate value, social pressure and practical constraints. Reflect briefly and ask one question per turn. Respect rejection of the frame; ambivalence is not a defect to repair. Do not use the exercise to manufacture commitment.
