---
description: Clarify values, goals, rules and felt rewards
argument-hint: "[domain]"
---
Explore ${@:-an interesting life domain} with examples and tradeoffs. Distinguish value (an ongoing direction), goal (an endpoint), rule (a demanded behavior), desired feeling, interpersonal expectation, and avoidance. Do not presume that every activity must serve a larger purpose. Ask about real situations when possible and retain uncertainty; use competing mundane explanations when a pattern looks too neat. Any chosen direction remains provisional unless the user commits to it.
