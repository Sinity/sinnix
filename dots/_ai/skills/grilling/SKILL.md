---
name: grilling
description: Grill the user relentlessly about a plan or design. Use when the user wants to stress-test a plan before building, or uses any 'grill' trigger phrases.
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

By default, ask one numbered round containing every independent question whose prerequisites are already settled. Do not include a question that depends on another answer in the same round. Wait for the round's answers, update the design tree, then ask the new frontier. If the user asks for one-at-a-time questioning or the chain is deeply serial, ask one question per turn instead.

If a _fact_ can be found by exploring the codebase, look it up rather than asking me. Continue non-blocked investigation while decisions are pending. The _decisions_, though, are mine: put each one to me and wait for my answer.

Do not enact the plan until I confirm we have reached a shared understanding.
