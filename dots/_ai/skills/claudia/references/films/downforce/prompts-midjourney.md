# Midjourney — every prompt (DOWNFORCE)

Model: **Midjourney v8.2**. Every prompt ran with `--v 8.2 --raw --hd` and anabology's personal profile (its code is left out), at `--ar 16:9`, except the full-length identity plate (rh-02 id-full) at `--ar 9:16`; no prompt had any other parameter, so none are shown after the prompts. No prompt carries a style reference or an image prompt, none ran in thinking mode, and nothing was animated in Midjourney: every image reaches the screen through a Wan 3.0 or Seedance 2.5 take (the takes and their prompts are in [prompts-video.md](https://claudia.gallery/record/downforce/prompts-video/)). No Midjourney image is shown as a still. The Midjourney images are not in the spend ledger.

**Counts** (measured for this package from the job files and the images on disk): **20 prompts in 2 batches**. rh-01-look is Claudia's look test: 6 prompts. rh-02-plates holds the plates for the video takes: 14 prompts. Every job came back with four images: **80 images**, all PNG, 577,743,636 bytes (578 MB) in total. 76 are 2944×1648 and the 4 of id-full are 1648×2944. The prompts run from 676 to 915 characters (median 828.5). **18 images were picked** as plates or references: 14, one per job of rh-02, and 4 more from the same batch for the round-2 plan. **14 of the 18 reached the final film.** claudia.gallery's gallery marks 12 of them as in the film: it leaves out g29 #4 and id-face #3 (see *Screened out, and why*). 38 video jobs started from these images or referenced them, either directly or through a frame of an earlier take. 19 of those takes are in the final film, in 40 of its shots. The look test's 24 images were never used as plates.

There are two delivered cuts. **The first cut** (v0) went out, and anabology's notes on it came. **The final film** (v1) went out, and anabology approved it: "this is perfect. done. nothing more to do". All 80 images were made before the first cut, and the re-shoots after the notes reused them.

Every job's kind in the job files is `plate`. The rh-02 jobs are named after the Wan generation each was made for (g03 is EYES, g05 is G-FORCE, and so on); id-full and id-face are the identity plates for Wan's video edits. "#2" means image 2 of that job (`<job>-2.png`). After a prompt, "plate: #2" marks a picked image and what it became, "not used" marks a pick that never reached the final film, and "shortlisted" names the candidates the lead set side by side before picking. Times written as 0:36.1 are times in the film, which is the song. The production lead picked the round-1 plates, and the round-2 plan's scouts and head director picked four more. anabology did not rank these images.

## How the prompts were written

Claude, the production lead, wrote the six look-test prompts. The twelve shot plates of rh-02 were drafted by the storyboard's head director, an agent of the storyboard workflow (`tools/build_board.py`), one for each Wan shot of Claudia on the board. Before submitting them, the lead compacted them and added the two identity plates.

Each prompt has the same shape: the framing, the canon, the wardrobe, the action and the set, a photographic tail, and the ending "no text, no letters, no logos". All 20 end that way. No prompt names Tesla, the Roadster or any brand.

- **Her, close and medium shots (and every rh-02 plate):** the canon from claudia.gallery's consistency guide, pasted verbatim: "a 28-year-old Caucasian American woman with a grown-up angular face, defined cheekbones and a strong jawline, pale skin and light freckles, a glossy black blunt jaw-length bob with heavy straight bangs and one clay-orange streak through the bangs, a small flat clay-orange eight-pointed star hair clip, a thin headset microphone at her cheek". Ethnicity and age are written out because Midjourney drifts her ethnicity, and because on the earlier films anabology rejected plates where she read young. Every plate was screened for both. No prompt adds a word about a country, a region or an ethnic style.
- **Her, full length (the look test's exit-smoke):** the canon's short form, also verbatim: "a Caucasian American woman in her late twenties with pale skin and a glossy black blunt bob with heavy straight bangs and one clay-orange streak, a thin headset microphone". In rh-02 the full-length identity plate (id-full) used the full canon at 9:16, and all four images still came back at full length.
- **One look for the whole film, the racing suit:** the look test asked for "wearing a fitted white racing suit with a sculpted high-fashion cut, matte white with thin clay-orange piping, a high collar, white racing gloves, no lettering on her clothes". The board's drafts added "closed to the throat" to the collar. As submitted in rh-02 it reads "wearing a fitted white racing suit with thin clay-orange piping and a high collar, white racing gloves, no lettering on her clothes", and id-full adds "white racing boots". The Wan prompts call it "her fitted white racing suit with thin clay-orange piping and a high collar closed to the throat, white racing gloves, no lettering on her clothes". "No lettering on her clothes" did not stop Midjourney from printing patches, badges and numbers on some suits (see the screening section).
- **The car and the cockpit:** the look test put her in "the cockpit of a white supercar" or "the low seat of a futuristic white wedge-shaped electric supercar". The look test drew a generic 1980s-style wedge car (below), so the board described a cockpit and no car: "inside a minimal modern cockpit at night, white leather and carbon, a yoke steering wheel, warm amber cockpit light on her face, thin red light strips outside the glass, black night". The submitted prompts compact this to "in a minimal white-and-carbon cockpit at night, a yoke, warm amber light on her face, red light strips outside the glass". The real demo car had nobody in it and its interior was never filmed, so her cockpit is invented. The red light strips are the colour of the real set.
- **Outside the car:** the wall portrait and the deadpan close-up add "no vehicle".
- **The tail:** "35mm film photograph, high-fashion motorsport editorial, film grain" in the look test (two of its prompts without "film grain"), compacted to "35mm film photograph, motorsport fashion editorial" in rh-02.

**Length.** Midjourney rewrites prompts longer than about 1,000 characters into its own wording; AIRPLANE MODE has no images from the eleven it rewrote. The board's twelve drafts ran from 813 to 1,057 characters, and three were over 1,000 (g03 at 1,057, g10 at 1,010, g05 at 1,001). Before submitting, the lead made the same three replacements in all twelve, for the suit, the cockpit and the tail (all quoted above). Ten prompts lost 142 characters each, the two without a cockpit lost 81, and the results ran from 732 to 915. None of the 20 prompts was rewritten.

**Upside down.** None of the look test's four upside-down images shows her hanging upside down; Midjourney only tilted her (sheet 09–12). So the plates for the upside-down shots asked for her weightless, "her black bob and bangs lifted straight up above her head as if weightless", and were turned 180° before Wan. Turned over, the lifted hair reads as hair hanging toward the roof.

## Screened out, and why

- **Age.** In the look test, 11 reads a bit young; it was never a plate. In rh-02, as the plan required, every plate was screened before use, and the four round-2 picks were recorded as adult. Some takes read younger than their plates. g03's take reads rounder and younger after about 4.5 s, and the film uses only its earlier windows. g19's take reads soft and young in its first second, and g25's face reads young; both were retired for other faults as well. g29's take reads young at its close size; the round-2 plan kept a $1 re-roll on #2 in reserve, but it was not put to anabology and was not run, and the take is in the film.
- **Off-model.** id-face #3 has a lettered collar badge, a white streak beside the clay one and a five-point star; it was dropped after round 1. g03 #2 and #3 have a white second streak (seen on the sheet). The frame that starts g05-r3 has two clay bands in the bangs and was kept.

- **Lips.** Every Wan prompt of her says "Her lips stay closed; she never talks." Every plate with parted lips (g05 #2, g19 #3, g24 #3) gave takes in which Wan opened her mouth. In round 2, g05 #3 and g24 #1 were rejected for parted lips.

- **The pose.** The look test's upside-down prompt never inverted her (09–12).

## Lessons

- **The canon text, verbatim.** Her identity is the canon text of claudia.gallery's consistency guide, pasted unchanged: the full form for every shot of her in rh-02, including the full-length identity plate, and the short form only in the look test's full-length shot. No prompt adds a word about a country, a region or an ethnic style. The look test's screen found her Caucasian and freckled in all 24 images, and adult in all but one (11, which reads a bit young). The faults the records name after that are in her marks and clothes, not her face's ethnicity: a second streak, a five-point star, lettered patches, cutouts. The recorded problems of age are in the video takes, which can read younger than their plates (see the screening section above).
- **Long prompts get rewritten.** Midjourney rewrites a prompt longer than about 1,000 characters into its own wording. The board's drafts were 813–1,057 characters; the lead cut the same three clauses from all twelve before submitting (732–915), and none was rewritten.
- **Midjourney cannot draw the real Roadster.** Asked for "a white wedge-shaped electric supercar", it drew a generic 1980s-style wedge. Nothing in the film is a Midjourney car. Her plates show an invented cockpit or "no vehicle", and every shot of her with the car is a Wan edit of Tesla's real footage, with the identity plate as the only reference for her.
- **Upside down by rotation.** Midjourney would not hang her upside down. A weightless plate turned 180° reads as upside down, and the video model supplies the falling hair (g39).
- **Closed lips in the plate.** A plate with parted lips gives a take in which she talks. From round 2 on, closed lips were part of the screen.
- **One reference in an edit.** With two identity images, Wan cut to the second one as a shot. Round 2 used the cleaned full-length plate alone.
