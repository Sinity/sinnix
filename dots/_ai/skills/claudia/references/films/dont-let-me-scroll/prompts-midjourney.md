# Midjourney — every prompt (DON'T LET ME SCROLL)

Model: **Midjourney v8.2**. Every prompt ran with `--v 8.2 --raw --hd` and anabology's personal profile (its code is left out), at the aspect ratio given with each prompt; no prompt carries a style reference. The three batches of v2 actions also carried an image prompt (an earlier pick, named under each batch). Her images reach the screen only through Wan 3.0 and Seedance 2.5 takes (their prompts are in [prompts-video.md](https://claudia.gallery/record/dont-let-me-scroll/prompts-video/)), apart from two face crops used as her avatar (P05, P21); the feed's pictures appear as the photographs and moving loops inside the phone's cards. The Midjourney images are not in the spend ledger.

**Counts** (measured for this package from the prompt records and the images): **130 prompts in 7 batches**. 129 jobs came back with four images each, **516 images**, all PNG, 3,414,792,325 bytes (3.41 GB); one job never came back (a36-lookback, below). The prompts run from 87 to 600 characters (median 417.5); every one of the 130 ends "no text, no letters, no logos". **Midjourney Animate** made the feed's moving loops: 40 submissions in two lists, 22 loops, two 5-second clips each (44 clips, 406 MB).

**What became of them, in short:** 110 images became first frames, references or the feed's pictures. 39 of the first frames and references reach the final cut through a take (the 54 feed pictures are a library the chapters draw on by kind, and were not traced shot by shot). anabology's picks and corrections, in their words, are at the end.

Times written as 0:55 are times in the song. "#2" means image 2 of a job (`<job>-2.png`).

## How the prompts were written

Claude, the production lead, wrote all 130, each at 600 characters or less; every prompt of her pastes her canon (below) word for word.

- **Her, close and medium shots:** the canon from claudia.gallery's consistency guide, pasted verbatim: "a 28-year-old Caucasian American woman with a grown-up angular face, defined cheekbones and a strong jawline, pale skin and light freckles, a glossy black blunt jaw-length bob with heavy straight bangs and one clay-orange streak through the bangs, a small flat clay-orange eight-pointed star hair clip, a thin headset microphone at her cheek". 24 prompts use it. Ethnicity and age are written out because Midjourney drifts her ethnicity and because anabology rejected young-looking plates on earlier films; every pick was screened for both, and no prompt adds a word about a country, a region or an ethnic style.
- **Her, full length:** the canon's short form, also verbatim: "a Caucasian American woman in her late twenties with pale skin and a glossy black blunt bob with heavy straight bangs and one clay-orange streak, a thin headset microphone". 47 prompts use it, every full-length plate among them; the 9:16 frame keeps her whole body at a high resolution for clean roto.
- **The backdrop:** every performance plate puts her "alone on a plain mid-grey seamless studio backdrop, soft even studio light", so the matting has a clean edge; the v2 stills say "on soft grey seamless paper, even soft light". The real bedroom's plates (R01–R35) are 16:9 and lit by her phone or the dawn.
- **The wardrobe, as written:** A "oversized white silk-satin pyjamas", B "oversized iridescent oil-slick satin pyjamas", C "an oversized acid-green mohair sweater, white sleep shorts", D "a black ribbed long-sleeve top and loose black satin trousers", with "a sharp black blazer" added for LinkedIn. In v2: the dream look first as "a cropped baby-pink mohair cardigan with pearl buttons and a pleated pearlescent lavender satin mini skirt" (anabology's first pick), then "an oversized white men's shirt, half-tucked into a short pleated lavender satin skirt" (and the black and white skirts); F "a red tailored coat dress with a wide white collar and a matching red beret, white knee socks, black patent shoes".
- **The feed:** 54 short prompts for the pictures a feed serves at 3 am: X photographs, reels, Pinterest pins, LinkedIn pictures and acid-blur photographs. No Claudia in them (she is in the film, not the feed) and no text: every interface, caption and count is drawn in code. The lyric's own references are in them only as places and things: the Gulf Coast at dusk, a stainless-steel rocket.
- **The v2 actions** carry an image prompt so the new moves keep a picked look: anabology's dream-look pick for the first three, the v1 plates of looks C and D (P14, P24) for the green dance and the black swipe, f3-red-2 for the red set, and s1-lavender-1 for the lavender re-run.

## Lessons

- **Check every pick large.** A third arm passed a screen on thumbnails about 230 px tall; at 800 px it is unmissable. Backwards legs passed the lead's screen of the stills too; anabology caught them on the take. Legs in a turn or a step need the same large look as hands.
- **Keep prompts at 600 characters or less.** Midjourney rewrites prompts past about 1,000 characters into its own wording; 600 kept every prompt well clear, and the shirt batch was shortened to fit.
- **Image prompts carry a look into new moves.** The actions in looks C, D and F took their outfits from a v1 plate or a picked look, and the takes made from them matched the swaps and the earlier shots.
- **Grey backdrops for anything that will be cut out.** Every performance plate was on plain mid-grey, and the matting kept her hair and the headset cable.
- **No image of her stands alone in the film, apart from her avatar crops.** Everything else of her moves, through Wan or Seedance; Midjourney's part is the first frame, the look and the feed around her.
